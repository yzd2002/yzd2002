# Zida Yang · 杨子达

I am a Ph.D. student in Management Science and Information Systems at the Guanghua School of Management, Peking University. I study how AI systems use evidence when information changes, when sources disagree, and when a decision has consequences in the physical world.

### Selected work

- [EMER-Guard](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1205/) checks emergency instructions against published protocols. *EMNLP 2026.*
- [Beyond Static Grounding](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1494/) tests how retrieval-augmented systems respond when their supporting evidence changes. *EMNLP 2026.*
- [EvoVLA](https://aigeeksgroup.github.io/EvoVLA/) examines how a robot can distinguish real progress from apparent success in a multi-step task. *ECCV 2026.*

Three papers have been accepted to **WSDM 2027 Findings**. [Titles and research questions](https://yzd2002.github.io/publications.html?type=accepted).
